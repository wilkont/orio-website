QUESTIONS THAT MATTER

Clarity before
participation.

What is ORIO intended to do?

ORIO is a proposed token-based economic layer for converting into, holding and redeeming value under explicit protocol rules. Its design connects eligible transaction fees to eligible holdings, while separating internal accounting from finalized on-chain ownership. These are intended functions, not a statement that live conversion, redemption, merchant acceptance or liquidity is available.

Who can participate, and in which countries?

No jurisdiction-specific eligibility or legal clearance is established by this preview. Country availability, participant requirements and any restrictions must be determined and documented through release-specific legal and compliance review before access is enabled. Being able to view this website does not mean you are eligible to use ORIO. This preview does not accept funds or applications to participate.

Is ORIO available for live financial use?

No live-money readiness has been verified for this preview. The foundational database work and local tests do not establish end-to-end production qualification.

Does “progressive” mean guaranteed appreciation?

No. It describes the proposed transaction-linked economic mechanism, not a guarantee of yield, return, liquidity, price stability or protection from loss. Activity, implementation, fees and risk all matter.

Is ORIO the same asset as USDC?

No. USDC is the selected initial master-asset direction for the ORIO design. ORIO's proposed economic layer is distinct. This site does not claim issuance by Circle, endorsement by Circle or reserve backing that has not been verified.

What needs to happen before activation?

Complete and verify the financial flows and deployed integrations, resolve remaining economic policies, and obtain release-specific legal, security, custody, reserve/financial and operational qualification.
