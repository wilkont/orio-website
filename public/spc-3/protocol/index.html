ORIO Protocol

Separate economics, custody and evidence.

PRE-LAUNCH Financial services are not enabled.

Execution

Qualification contracts include currency, monetary controller, reserve vault, redemption locks, master-asset registry and Gas Treasury paymaster candidates. They reject Base mainnet.

Two ledgers

Ledger A records immutable balanced financial journals and entitlements. Ledger B records finalized on-chain ownership and execution. Reconciliation links the two and rejects duplicate external events.

Failure behavior

Invalid, stale or conflicting evidence must pause the affected settlement rather than create unsupported balances. Accounting corrections use compensating entries.
