Risk disclosures

Progress does not eliminate risk.

PRE-LAUNCH Financial services are not enabled.

Economic and asset risk

Activity-linked appreciation is not guaranteed. Stablecoins can depeg, reserves can be impaired and markets can lack liquidity. Positive residual supply can concentrate fee allocations. Tests do not eliminate strategic behavior or market risk.

Technical and operational risk

Contracts, signing systems, providers, bridges, recovery procedures and networks can fail. Network fees can be charged on failed operations. Recovery and chain finality have delays and assumptions.

Legal and participation risk

Eligibility and permitted services vary by jurisdiction. No live SPC 3 eligibility, custody arrangement, insurance or production reserve attestation is established here. Do not send funds in anticipation of a launch.
