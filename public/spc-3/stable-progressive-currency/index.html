Stable Progressive Currency

A reference price, not a guaranteed peg.

PRE-LAUNCH Financial services are not enabled.

What progressive means

The monetary design allocates eligible conversion fees to remaining holders through the redemption price. This is an economic mechanism, not guaranteed interest, investment performance or protection against loss.

Genesis

The original opening reference is $1 per ORIO. It is not a perpetual $1 promise. USDC must be valued using reserve controls; a dollar peg must not simply be assumed.

Availability

Live purchases and redemptions are not available. Test-network connectivity and local execution evidence do not establish production readiness.
