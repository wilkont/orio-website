Privacy information

A website without wallet enrollment.

PRE-LAUNCH Financial services are not enabled.

Inputs

The fee explorer runs in the browser and does not send its input to an application service. This release has no registration, wallet-connection or payment form and requests no recovery secrets.

Hosting and contact

A hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP address and browser metadata. Emailing the project shares your message and address with its mail systems. Do not send financial credentials or sensitive account data.

Before enrollment

Detailed controller contact, retention, jurisdictional rights, identity-processing and account privacy information must be finalized before customer onboarding. This site does not claim that a future wallet has already met those requirements.
