Pricing and fees

Conversion and network fees are separate.

PRE-LAUNCH Financial services are not enabled.

Conversion fee

Purchases and redemptions carry the mandatory 1% conversion fee. Ordinary allocation is 20% company and 80% eligible holders before final asset-unit rounding. Zero-holder purchases and full-supply redemptions allocate the entire fee to the company.

Network fee

Users pay the applicable network gas for purchases, redemptions, transfers and other authorized on-chain operations. The company Gas Treasury is intended to pay the network on their behalf. Gas does not create appreciation or conversion revenue.

Limits and credits

A transaction must not exceed its authorized maximum gas charge without renewed consent. Unused estimates must be refunded or credited. Platform-caused failure charging needs an approved disclosed policy; no live fee schedule beyond the protocol rules is offered here.
