Purchase disclosure

The intended confirmation separates purchase amount, 1% conversion fee, network fee, token quantity, expiry and maximum authorized cost. Network costs must not reduce the monetary backing silently.

Redemption disclosure

A redemption confirmation must show gross value, conversion fee, network charge and final proceeds. Any gas deduction from proceeds requires authorization.

Not a live quote

The homepage fee explorer is illustrative approved-policy arithmetic. It does not accept funds, estimate actual network fees or execute conversions.
