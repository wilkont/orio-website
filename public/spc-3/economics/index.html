Monetary economics

The approved rules, made explicit.

PRE-LAUNCH Financial services are not enabled.

Ordinary conversions

Purchases and redemptions charge 1%. Company entitlement is rounded down from 20% of the collected fee and holders receive the remainder. Purchase appreciation occurs before minting; redeemed tokens are excluded from their own appreciation.

Zero holders and full redemption

When supply is zero, the entire purchase fee goes to the company. Full-supply redemption also sends the entire fee to the company. There is no genesis or residual-supply escrow.

Precision and residual supply

Minting and stablecoin payouts round down. The conversion fee rounds up to the settlement unit. Rounding residuals are explicit and segregated. Every positive remaining supply receives the ordinary holder share, even if tiny; unsafe precision must reject settlement.

Reopening

A fully redeemed supply retains its last valid reference price. New issuance waits for reconciliation and does not automatically reset the reference to $1.
