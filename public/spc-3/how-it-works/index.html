How ORIO works

Understand the flow of value.

PRE-LAUNCH Financial services are not enabled.

Purchase

The approved model charges a 1% conversion fee. With existing eligible holders, 20% of the collected fee goes to ORIO Company and the remainder appreciates eligible holdings before new tokens are minted. No extra ORIO is minted to distribute appreciation.

Hold and send

Ordinary ORIO transfers have no additional protocol fee. Every on-chain action still carries a separately quoted, user-paid network fee. Company-managed execution is intended to remove the need for users to hold ETH.

Redeem

A redemption batch uses a common opening price. Redeemed tokens do not receive appreciation from their own redemption. Full-supply redemption allocates the entire conversion fee to the company.
