Governance

Changes need accountable authorization.

PRE-LAUNCH Financial services are not enabled.

Monetary policy

The founder-approved economics control implementation. Material economic changes require explicit authorization; an operator must not silently redirect holder allocations.

Reserve changes

A master-asset replacement must preserve supply and holder balances, disclose losses, and not manufacture conversion fees or appreciation. Qualification code and accounting replay cover this boundary.

Release acceptance

Legal, security, custody, financial and operational acceptance bind a specific release and deployment. One accountable owner may cover multiple roles; external review remains advisory. No public governance voting service is active.
