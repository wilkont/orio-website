ORIO Wallet

Simple access requires careful recovery.

PRE-LAUNCH Financial services are not enabled.

Intended experience

The wallet is designed to show balances, complete transaction costs, authorization limits and reconciled receipts without requiring users to manage ETH. Consumer account enrollment is not open.

Recovery candidate

Qualification contracts support multiple guardians, a quorum, a recovery delay, owner cancellation and execution freezing during a pending recovery. Consumer enrollment, notifications and recovery operations still require integration.

Protect your credentials

Never give anyone a recovery phrase, private key or account password. This website does not request wallet connections or recovery secrets.
